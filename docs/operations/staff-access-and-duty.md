# Staff access and duty check-in (staff manual note)

**Applies to:** approved ASO, SO, SSO and DSE staff (and the other shift-based roles).

## What you can do before you check in

Sign in and open every page your role already allows: your workspace, report history and lookup, report forms, leave, roster, notifications, the Bay Board, weather and the other read-only pages. You can also fill in a report form and **save a draft**; a saved draft opens on your other devices.

Your role, station, entity and account approval still decide what you can see. Signing in off duty never widens access. A pending, inactive or revoked account is still refused, and you still cannot open another station's data.

## What needs an open Duty Check-In

Each active report form shows **"Check in to submit"** with a link to Duty Check-In while you are off duty. These need an open check-in, and the system refuses them without one, even if the request is made another way:

- submitting any security report (SEC013, SEC014, SEC016, SEC018, SEC029, SEC033, Makeup, Breakup);
- physical work: starting or finishing an aircraft search, processing a CaterLink checkpoint, completing a shift handover.

Check-in and check-out still control your attendance and overtime.

## Not a lock-out

Older training text that says staff are held at the Duty Check-In page until they check in is out of date. Staff are no longer redirected; they are only refused at the moment they try to submit or do physical work.
